# The Repo Line (Transit Map, vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps, ~44 s)

Candidates: (1) single journey: one red line from the Reserve Bank to your bond; (2) a network fanning
out from one interchange (banks) into four household lines; (3) countdown to 19 Nov. Chosen: (2) with
(3) as the ending: the rate decision is the source, banks the interchange, every household cost a line.

Logline: one number set in Pretoria travels, station by station, into your monthly budget.
Arc: setup (SARB, inflation river) -> turn (banks interchange falls, four lines fan out) -> ending
(bond terminus R10,152; next stop 19 Nov drawn dashed; whole network with the MMM title).

Benchmark: classic metro-map motion explainers. Learn: one line at a time, pauses on stations.
Don't take: any real network layout, bullets or fonts.

Facts (FACTS.md): repo 7.25% and prime 10.75% after the 23 Sep 2026 hike (SAnews/EWN reports);
CPI 4.5% Sep 2026 (Stats SA P0141); R1m 20y at 10.75% = R10,152/m; at 10.50% = R9,984 (+R168 per 0.25%);
next MPC 19 Nov 2026 (businessnewssouthafrica.co.za 28 Oct 2026).

Simplified for this trial: 2D top-down canvas with paper tooth, eased camera pans/zooms; no WebGL tilt or DOF.
English only (MMM's language), captions burned in for sound-off viewing.
