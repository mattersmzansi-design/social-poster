# Who leads the metros, 2000–2026: facts and sources

1920x1080, 36 s, chart only, soft procedural music (tools/music_soft.py), no voice. Style follows the owner's "Registration Gap" example.

Metro councils were created in 2000 (1995/96 had transitional councils), so the series starts in 2000.
Buffalo City and Mangaung became metros in 2011; their 2000 and 2006 figures are for their local councils (marked on screen).
The chart shows the LARGEST party's seats, which is not always the governing party (e.g. Johannesburg 2016: ANC largest, DA-led coalition governed).
